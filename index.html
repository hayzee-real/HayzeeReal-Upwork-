01 Pronoun reference
Original

Knowing that you have something to contribute is not arrogance; that is confidence.

Edit

Knowing that you have something to contribute is not arrogance; it is confidence.

Margin comment

“That” has to reach back over “arrogance” to find what it means, so for a moment the sentence appears to say that arrogance is confidence. “It” attaches straight to “knowing that you have something to contribute,” which is what you mean.

02 Meaning restored
Original

Your needs may sometimes conflict with those you supervise or even those who supervise you.

Edit

Your needs may sometimes conflict with those of the people you supervise, or even with those who supervise you.

Margin comment

As written, your needs conflict with the people rather than with their needs, which is not what the sentence means. “Those of” restores the comparison the sentence is making.

03 One comma
Original

Although we may want to identify with others’ pain, as we naturally should to bring encouragement, this is often a difficult, if not an impossible task, since we are not walking in their shoes.

Edit

Although we may want to identify with others’ pain, as we naturally should to bring encouragement, this is often a difficult, if not an impossible, task, since we are not walking in their shoes.

Margin comment

One comma added. The interruption “if not an impossible” needs closing so that both “difficult” and “impossible” attach cleanly to “task.” Your wording is untouched.

04 One comma
Original

We must ask ourselves has leadership forgotten what it felt like to be an employee?

Edit

We must ask ourselves, has leadership forgotten what it felt like to be an employee?

Margin comment

One comma. The question needs an introduction; without it the two halves run together and the reader has to go back.

05 One word removed
Original

A good leader will structure a situation so that the employee makes the choice that best meets the needs of both the individual and of the company.

Edit

A good leader will structure a situation so that the employee makes the choice that best meets the needs of both the individual and the company.

Margin comment

One word removed. “Both” already governs the two items, so the second “of” leaves the sentence with one preposition too many. It now reads “the needs of both the individual and the company.”

06 Repetition removed
Original

I only know that years later, when I read this chapter, I still feel the weight of knowing what happened after our conversation.

Edit

I only know that years later, when I read this chapter, I still feel the weight of what happened after our conversation.

Margin comment

“Know” and “knowing” sit twelve words apart in the same sentence. Removing the second one loses nothing, because “the weight of what happened” already carries the meaning.

07 Echo between sentences
Original

Not every question or comment deserves your immediate yes-or-no agreement or disagreement. You don't always have to have the answer immediately.

Edit

Not every question or comment deserves your immediate yes-or-no agreement or disagreement. You don’t always have to have the answer straight away.

Margin comment

“Immediate” and “immediately” land in consecutive sentences, which makes the second one sound like an echo rather than a new point. “Straight away” carries the same meaning and lets the second sentence stand on its own.

08 Repetition in a list
Original

It is still important to prepare both the present and the next generation for the future. Providing training, networking opportunities, mentorship, and opportunities to grow helps keep organizations healthy.

Edit

It is still important to prepare both the present and the next generation for the future. Providing training, networking, mentorship, and room to grow helps keep organizations healthy.

Margin comment

“Opportunities” appears twice inside one four-item list, which makes the list sound as though it is doubling back on itself. “Networking” and “room to grow” carry the same meaning without the repetition.

09 Restatement cut
Original

People still matter, and that does not mean we cannot use technology, systems, and processes to become more efficient. We can use technology to improve efficiency, but we still need people to lead, think, serve, communicate, and make sound decisions.

Edit

People still matter, and that does not mean we cannot use technology, systems, and processes to become more efficient. But we still need people to lead, think, serve, communicate, and make sound decisions.

Margin comment

The first sentence says technology can make us more efficient. The second said it again before turning to the point. Cutting the restatement lets the contrast land directly on what you want the reader to carry away.

10 Run-up removed
Original

When I look at the people around me now, I see that leadership is never just about the person making the decision. It is about the lives that decision will influence for years to come.

Edit

Leadership is never just about the person making the decision. It is about the lives that decision will influence for years to come.

Margin comment

This paragraph and the one before it make the same point: that decisions today shape what is available to others tomorrow. I have kept both, because the earlier one is personal and this one is the principle, but removing the run-up lets this land as a statement rather than as an observation about a statement. It is the last idea before your closing lines, and it should arrive without preamble.

11 Seven items into three
Original

I grew up professionally in what today might be described as network operations, network administration, telecommunications management, IT infrastructure, communications systems management, network engineering, or even network architecture.

Edit

I grew up professionally in what today might be described as network operations and administration, telecommunications and infrastructure management, or network engineering and architecture.

Margin comment

Seven items is more than a reader can hold, and the word “network” appears four times inside them. The point of the sentence is the breadth of the field rather than the individual job titles, and grouping them into three pairs makes that breadth easier to see, not harder. If any one of these titles matters to you personally, tell me, and I will keep it standing on its own.

FAQ

Questions authors ask me most

Cannot find your answer? Message me on WhatsApp or email and I will reply within 24 hours.

How do I know which edit my book needs?

Send me 1,000 words and I will edit them free. Along with the sample, I will tell you which level your manuscript is actually at, even when the honest answer costs me the bigger job.

Most business books that ask for a copy edit need a developmental pass first, because the problem is the argument rather than the sentences. Paying to polish a structure that is not working is the most common way authors waste money on a book.

What is the difference between developmental, line and copy editing?

Developmental editing works on the book: structure, argument, chapter order, what each chapter is for, and whether the whole thing delivers what the introduction promised. Line editing works on the prose: rhythm, clarity and voice. Copy editing works on correctness: grammar, punctuation, consistency, style sheet, and name and fact checking.

For a first book the usual path is all three, in that order. Doing them in one pass sounds efficient and is not, because line work gets thrown away the moment a chapter moves.

Will my book still sound like me?

Yes, and that is the point. Your voice is the asset you are selling. My job is to remove whatever is standing in front of it, usually the corporate register people slip into the moment they start writing rather than talking.

Every change is tracked, every judgment call carries a margin comment, and the final decision on all of it is yours. One client wrote that the edit improved the structure, flow and clarity of her manuscript while keeping her voice and message completely intact.

My book has to bring in business. Can you edit for that?

That is the frame I work in. A developmental edit from me asks what your reader can do differently by the end, whether your framework is teachable on the page, and whether the book earns the call to action you want at the back of it. A book that reads beautifully and converts nobody is a failed business asset.

Can you edit a book I drafted with ChatGPT or another AI tool?

Yes. It is one of the most common requests I get now. AI drafts tend to repeat themselves, pad every point and flatten the author into a generic voice. I cut the repetition and filler, tighten the argument chapter by chapter, flag where your own stories, examples and data need to go, and keep one consistent voice throughout, which is yours.

A client whose nonfiction draft I edited on exactly this brief wrote that I did not just point out what needed work, I explained why and gave practical suggestions for improvement.

What exactly do I receive?

A Microsoft Word file with native tracked changes and margin comments written in plain English rather than editorial shorthand, plus a clean copy on request. Line and copy editing projects include a style sheet built for your book.

Developmental projects add an editorial letter, a chapter by chapter assessment, a chapter map and a phased revision plan you can act on without guessing what to do first.

How long does it take?

It depends on length and service. As a rough guide, a 60,000-word line and copy edit takes about three weeks and an assessment about a week and a half. Use the estimator in the Process section for your own word count.

You get a real date in writing before you pay, and I deliver chapter by chapter so you are never waiting until the end to see what I am doing.

My manuscript is not finished. Is it too early to talk?

No. If you have three or four chapters drafted and an outline for the rest, an assessment at that point can save you from writing thirty thousand words you would later cut. For a coach turning a method into a book, this is often the best moment to bring an editor in.

I am a coach, not a writer. Is that a problem?

No. It is the normal starting point. Most of my clients have never been edited before, which is why my margin comments are written in plain language and kept short unless there is a real decision to make. You do not need the terminology. You need someone who will tell you the truth about the draft and then help you fix it.

How many rounds of revision are included?

One full editorial pass, then a follow-up review of the chapters you revise in response to it, included in the quote. If you later rewrite the book substantially, that is a new edit and I will quote it as one rather than quietly absorb it and rush.

What if I am not happy with the edit?

Tell me early. Because I deliver chapter by chapter, you see my approach on chapter one before I have touched chapter twelve, and if the register is wrong we correct it there. In twenty-four completed contracts this has never gone beyond a conversation.
